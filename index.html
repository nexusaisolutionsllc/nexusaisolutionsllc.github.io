Healthcare

Healthcare R&D Kit

An AI orchestration framework that deploys autonomous agents across the entire drug discovery pipeline. Traditional pharmaceutical R&D is one of the most resource-intensive processes in any industry — it typically takes 10 to 15 years and costs upwards of $2 billion to bring a single drug to market. Much of that time is spent on manual literature review, physical compound screening, and navigating fragmented datasets spread across genomic databases, chemical patent libraries, and electronic health records.

The Healthcare R&D Kit changes this equation. Our system ingests these massive, fragmented datasets simultaneously, cross-referencing findings across sources that researchers would normally spend months connecting manually. It uses multi-agent AI orchestration to identify novel drug targets, generate optimized molecular structures, predict compound viability, and even draft regulatory-ready documentation — all while maintaining the scientific rigor that pharmaceutical development demands.

  • Simultaneously ingests and cross-references genomic databases, chemical patent libraries, clinical trial data, and electronic health records
  • Generates optimized molecular structures and identifies novel drug targets using multi-agent AI orchestration
  • Predicts compound viability and drug-target interactions before physical screening, reducing costly lab iterations
  • Automates drafting of FDA regulatory submissions, clinical documentation, and compliance reports
  • Targeting a 50–70% reduction in the traditional 10–15 year drug development timeline

Media & Entertainment

Nexus Dubber

AI-powered dubbing that doesn't flatten a performance. When a film crosses borders, traditional dubbing replaces the actor's voice with a stranger's — and in the process, it loses the subtlety, emotion, and personality that made the performance work. The result is a version of the film that technically speaks the right language but emotionally speaks a different one. Audiences notice, and engagement drops.

Nexus Dubber takes a fundamentally different approach. Our AI preserves the original actor's voice signature — their unique timbre, cadence, breathing patterns, and emotional delivery — while adapting the dialogue for a new language. The system handles lip-sync alignment and natural speech rhythm automatically, so the dubbed version doesn't just sound translated, it sounds performed. Studios get global distribution at a fraction of traditional dubbing costs and timelines, without sacrificing the creative integrity that defines their work.

  • Preserves the actor's original voice signature, timbre, breathing patterns, and emotional delivery across every language
  • Automatic lip-sync alignment and natural speech rhythm adaptation — the dubbed version looks and feels native
  • Scales from a single source to multi-language distribution, enabling simultaneous global releases
  • Dramatically reduces dubbing costs and turnaround times compared to traditional voice-actor re-recording
  • Production-grade output trusted by major independent film studios for theatrical and streaming releases

Accessibility

Nexus Access

Over one billion people worldwide live with some form of disability, yet most accessibility tools are still workarounds rather than solutions. Screen readers describe what's on screen but can't act on it. Switch controls let you navigate an interface, but laboriously, one element at a time. These tools accommodate the computer's limitations — they don't transcend them.

Nexus Access takes a fundamentally different approach. It uses large language models to actually understand full computer environments — the operating system, running applications, open documents, browser tabs, and everything in between. Users interact through natural voice commands ("open my email and reply to the latest message from Sarah") and integrated eye-tracking for precise cursor control and selection. The AI interprets intent, not just words, and executes complex multi-step actions autonomously. The computer becomes an extension of the user's intent, not an obstacle to it.

  • LLM-powered deep understanding of any computer environment, application, and on-screen context
  • Natural voice command execution — speak in full sentences and natural language, not rigid command syntax or keyword triggers
  • Integrated eye-tracking for precise cursor control, element selection, and gaze-based scrolling
  • Executes complex multi-step actions autonomously — from composing emails to managing files to browsing the web
  • Works across operating systems and applications — not limited to specific software, browsers, or platforms
